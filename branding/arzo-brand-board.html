Logo usage

Do

  • Keep one gold accent per screen — the hero number or one key action.
  • Use ivory “A” on jade, jade “A” on light.
  • Give the lockup clear space equal to the height of the “A”.

Don't

  • Recolour outside the palette or add shadows/gradients to the mark.
  • Stretch, skew, or rotate the icon.
  • Make gold a fill colour everywhere — it stops being special.
